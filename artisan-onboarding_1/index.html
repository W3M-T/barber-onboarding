Questions

Asked at every onboarding.

Who do I contact for training support?

Charlie is your contact for anything on the training site — access problems, which modules apply to you, or future development opportunities.

Where do I find the handbook?

A link is attached to your Step I Handbook Meeting on the Meetings screen. You'll acknowledge it there once you've reviewed it.

Do I have to attend all four meetings?

Steps I, II, and IV apply to everyone. Step III — the Assistant Stylist Program introduction — is assigned only when it's relevant to your role.

Is the training tracked here?

No. This platform confirms your introduction and your access. Lesson-by-lesson progress lives on the separate training site.

What should I bring on day one?

A valid ID for your I-9, your banking details for direct deposit, and any license or certification your role requires.
